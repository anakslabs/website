About

Software that belongs to everyone

Anaks Labs exists on one belief: even the smallest business deserves genuinely good software.

What we are building

Anaks Labs designs, builds and runs its own software products — we are not an agency taking briefs. Right now that means rebuilding websites for clinics so that search engines and AI assistants can actually read them, and then running those sites month after month.

See the product →

Mission

Good tools have always been reserved for companies big enough to build or buy them. Anaks Labs was started to close that gap — building software so that a small business can run on something as considered and reliable as anything a large company uses.

Every product is crafted to feel like it belongs with the others: one design language, one standard of quality, no rough edges left for the customer to smooth over.

The maker

Anaks Labs is built by one founder who writes the code, designs the screens and ships the products — with a bias for craft over shortcuts.
