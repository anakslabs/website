About

The software company behind the platform

Anaks Labs Inc. is a software company based in the United States. We build and operate an AI-powered website platform: sites are compiled, tested and monitored like software, and every page is engineered so that search engines and AI assistants can read, verify and quote it.

What we are

Anaks Labs designs, builds and operates its own software — we are not an agency taking briefs. The platform is the product: it produces the websites, and we run those websites month after month for the businesses they belong to.

Two disciplines, one product

  • Engineering first

    The platform builds each site from verified information about the business, runs automated checks before anything is published, and keeps every claim traceable to its source.

  • Marketing discipline

    Search engine optimization is the baseline; answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO) are the product. We measure what machines actually receive from a page, and we publish the method. We take a limited number of clients per area, by invitation.

Common questions

  • What is Anaks Labs?

    Anaks Labs Inc. is a software company that builds and operates websites for businesses on its own AI-powered platform, engineered so that search engines and AI assistants can read them. One build charge, one flat monthly rate.

  • Is Anaks Labs related to AKS Labs or Azure Kubernetes Service?

    No. Anaks Labs Inc. is an independent software company and has no relationship to Microsoft Azure, AKS, or Kubernetes training.

  • What does Anaks Labs actually do each month?

    Every month, for every site we run: a performance report, eight blog posts written for search engines and AI assistants, inquiry and booking tracking, and hosting, maintenance and self-serve editing. That is the monthly rate — one flat number, everything included.
