Our services

What we do, what it involves, and how long you should expect to be with us.

Check-ups and cleaning

A full examination, a scale and polish, and a written plan for anything we find. Most visits take about forty minutes.

Fillings and crowns

Tooth-coloured fillings placed in a single visit. Crowns are milled to shape and fitted the same week.

Root canal treatment

Carried out under local anaesthetic, usually across two appointments. We book them long so nothing is rushed.

Whitening

A fitted tray and gel you take home, with a check-in after the first week to make sure the shade is going where you want it.

Implants

Planned from a 3D scan, placed in the practice, and restored once the site has healed. We talk through the whole timeline first.

Emergency visits

Pain, a lost filling or a broken tooth. We keep slots free every weekday and on Saturday mornings for exactly this.

Questions patients ask

Answered here, on the page, so that a search engine or an assistant can quote the answer instead of guessing at it.

How much does a check-up cost?

A first examination is charged as a single fee that covers the exam, any x-rays taken on the day, and the written plan you leave with. We tell you the figure when you book, not after the appointment.

How long does a filling take?

About half an hour for a single tooth, including the anaesthetic taking effect. You can eat as soon as the numbness wears off.

Can I be seen the same day if I am in pain?

Yes. We hold slots free every weekday and on Saturday mornings for pain, a lost filling or a broken tooth. Email us in the morning and we will tell you the earliest time we have.

Is there parking?

There is parking behind the building, free while you are with us. The entrance is step-free from the car park.
